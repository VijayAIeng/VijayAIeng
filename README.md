## Selected Projects

### Shabdh | Multilingual Voice Cloning
A zero-shot voice-cloning project focused on speaker representation and multilingual speech generation. The work explores how to preserve speaker identity while generating speech across languages.

**Technology:** PyTorch, XTTS-v2, ASR, TTS

### EMOD | Expressive Speech
Research into zero-shot emotion transfer for text-to-speech. The approach investigates disentangled representations for controlling expressive delivery without separately fine-tuning for every emotion.

**Technology:** PyTorch, StyleTTS2

### Enterprise RAG Platform
A retrieval-augmented generation design combining dense retrieval with graph-based context. The architecture includes role-based access control and audit logging for enterprise document workflows.

**Technology:** LangChain, Weaviate, Neo4j

### Streaming Conversational Agent
A voice interaction pipeline that connects automatic speech recognition, an LLM, and neural speech synthesis through a streaming backend. The focus is on coordinating the stages and reducing response delay.

**Technology:** FastAPI, LangGraph, WebSockets
